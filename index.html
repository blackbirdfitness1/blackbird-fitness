Meet the Founder

Founded by Hayden Blackbird
Indigenous Athlete | Lakeland College Soccer Player | Centre Back

I am from Beardy’s & Okemasis Cree Nation and I created Blackbird Fitness to help people become stronger, healthier, and more confident through fitness.

My journey as a college soccer player taught me the importance of discipline, teamwork, resilience, and believing in yourself. As a centre back, I learned that leadership is about protecting your team, staying focused, and pushing through challenges.

Blackbird Fitness combines my passion for soccer, fitness, and helping others. My goal is to support Indigenous communities, inspire young athletes, and help people create healthier lifestyles through affordable training they can do anywhere.

Whether your goal is becoming a better athlete or simply becoming a healthier version of yourself, Blackbird Fitness is here to help you grow.

Our Mission

Blackbird Fitness was created to help people build confidence, improve their health, and create a stronger lifestyle through fitness.

As an Indigenous athlete and Lakeland College soccer player, my goal is to inspire communities, support healthier choices, and help young athletes believe in themselves on and off the field.
