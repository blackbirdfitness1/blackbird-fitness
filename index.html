Our Mission

Blackbird Fitness was founded to help people build confidence, improve their health, and create stronger communities through fitness.

Created by an Indigenous college soccer player from Beardy's & Okemasis Cree Nation, our goal is to inspire healthier lifestyles while helping young athletes reach their full potential.
