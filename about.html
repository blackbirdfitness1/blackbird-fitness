Meet the Founder

Founded by Hayden Blackbird

I am an Indigenous athlete from Beardy’s & Okemasis Cree Nation and a former Lakeland College soccer player. As a centre back, I learned that success comes through discipline, leadership, and commitment.

I created Blackbird Fitness to help people build confidence, improve their health, and create positive habits through affordable fitness and soccer training.

My mission is to encourage healthier lifestyles, inspire young athletes, and support Indigenous communities by showing that fitness can be a powerful tool for personal growth.
